# Ricardian Service Contracts for M5-CV / SOPHIA Roles

> **Status:** Public Review Draft. This specification is not legal, tax, employment, securities, or payments advice.

A service engagement may connect holder-selected M5-CV evidence and a SOPHIA role to a separately negotiated Ricardian agreement. A capability claim, role label, credential, or agent recommendation is not itself a contract.

A conforming contract may synchronize:

1. `agreement.md` — human-readable terms;
2. `agreement.jsonld` — machine-readable meaning; and
3. `execution.json` — explicitly permitted executable actions.

All representations carry the same identifier, version, and digest; executable code may not silently change the human-readable legal agreement.

Automation may draft, calculate, notify, verify receipts, or prepare a proposed state change within a bounded delegation. Approval is required for actions that the agreement, policy, or applicable law reserves to an accountable person/entity.

Any M5AGT remains attributable to a current M5HUM/legal principal and subject to the M5Canon Six-Gate model.
