# M5isYOU — Human Capability Passport

> **Status: Public Review Draft for the people's review.** M5isYOU is a proposed human-first capability and contribution format. It is not an employment application, professional license, identity credential, promise of work or automated hiring system.

## The purpose

M5isYOU starts with a person rather than a résumé, curriculum vitae, school, former title or applicant-tracking score.

The public promise is:

> **No résumé filter. No invisible score. Start with the person, see the evidence, and create a pathway to contribute, demonstrate or learn.**

A person creates one legible page describing:

- the change they want to help create;
- what they can contribute now;
- evidence from paid, unpaid, community, caregiving, educational, creative or lived experience;
- problems they know how to solve;
- how they work best;
- what they want to learn;
- where they want to contribute; and
- what they authorize M5 to use and share.

M5isYOU is designed to help a person discover possible pathways into public contribution, cohorts, learning, grants, work samples and separately authorized operating roles. It must not silently decide a person's worth or future.

## Two synchronized forms

Each profile has:

1. a one-page human-readable view; and
2. a structured record confirmed by the person.

The person-readable page is the primary experience. Structured data exists to improve portability and transparent matching, not to replace the person's voice.

The proposed [one-page template](templates/M5ISYOU-ONE-PAGE.md) can be completed in plain text or through an accessible interface. The [capability-profile schema](schemas/m5isyou-capability-profile.schema.json) defines a portable record. Neither format is a verifiable credential until a separately authorized issuer verifies a specific claim and creates a credential for that limited purpose.

## The M5isYOU page

### 1. The change I want to help create

One sentence in the person's own words.

### 2. What I can contribute now

Choose up to seven capabilities and add a proof sentence to the most important ones. A person may always add a capability the vocabulary does not contain.

#### Build, design and test

- **I can build** — software, hardware, systems, products or organizations.
- **I can design** — experiences, processes, services, policies or architectures.
- **I can make** — create physical, digital, artistic or practical things.
- **I can tinker and prototype** — explore unfamiliar tools and turn ideas into early working forms.
- **I can test and challenge** — find defects, test assumptions and verify whether something works safely.
- **I can repair** — diagnose failures and restore systems, devices or processes.
- **I can secure** — protect systems, identities, wallets, communications and data.

#### Strategy, sales and relationships

- **I can strategize** — see the larger system, set direction and translate goals into action.
- **I can sell** — communicate value, develop customers and close appropriate opportunities.
- **I can create partnerships** — connect people and institutions, negotiate alignment and maintain trusted relationships.
- **I can connect** — identify the people, resources and opportunities that belong together.
- **I can communicate** — write, speak, translate, document or tell the story.

#### Leadership, operations and support

- **I can lead** — establish direction, make accountable decisions and support a team.
- **I can organize** — coordinate people, milestones, resources and operations.
- **I can operate** — run workflows reliably and resolve day-to-day issues.
- **I can support** — help members, customers, teams and communities succeed.
- **I can teach** — explain, demonstrate, coach or develop curricula.
- **I can steward** — care for shared resources, standards, knowledge or community trust.
- **I can govern** — facilitate decisions, manage conflicts, document authority and support accountable communities.

#### Research, law, ledgers and markets

- **I can research** — investigate, compare evidence and communicate findings.
- **I can analyze** — work with data, economics, markets, risk or complex systems.
- **I can ledger** — maintain books, reconcile records, establish provenance and preserve auditable transaction histories.
- **I can work with law** — conduct legal research, organize documents, analyze jurisdictional requirements or support qualified counsel.
- **I understand traditional finance** — payments, accounting, banking, securities, commercial instruments or markets.
- **I understand digital assets and DeFi** — wallets, protocols, liquidity, tokenized assets, smart contracts or decentralized markets.
- **I can bridge traditional finance and DeFi** — translate instruments, controls and settlement between both systems.
- **I can trade or support markets** — research, execution, market making, treasury, liquidity, operations or surveillance, subject to authorization.
- **I can manage risk** — identify exposure and develop controls, insurance, bonding or recovery strategies.

### 3. What I have actually done

Provide up to three examples. Evidence may include code, products, research, businesses, community work, teaching, caregiving, trades, creative work, volunteer service or a work sample.

A person may write **discuss privately** instead of publishing sensitive evidence.

### 4. Problems I know how to solve

Choose or describe up to three problems. These are written as outcomes rather than prior job titles.

### 5. How I work best

The person may describe collaboration style, type of problems, availability, work location and communication preferences. Accessibility accommodations are requested through a separate protected channel and are never used for matching or ranking.

### 6. What I want to learn

Choose up to three capabilities and identify whether mentoring, a cohort, a work sample or structured learning would help.

### 7. Where I want to contribute

Choose one or more role families from the [initial capability cards](M5ISYOU-ROLE-CAPABILITY-CARDS.md). Interest is not a claim of qualification and no match is an appointment.

### 8. My sharing choices

The person chooses:

- which fields may be used for matching;
- which approved reviewer groups may see them;
- whether they may be considered for compensated opportunities;
- whether the profile may be retained for later opportunities;
- an expiration date; and
- how to correct, export or delete the profile.

The default is private and time-limited.

## Finance and markets

People may describe their experience without being forced into one system:

- traditional finance;
- digital assets and DeFi;
- both; or
- learning.

Optional specialties include accounting and ledgers, payments, settlement, treasury, trading, market research, liquidity, commercial paper, bonds, tokenized assets, wallets, custody, smart contracts, risk, insurance, bonding, compliance and market integrity.

A selection indicates experience or interest. It does not establish a license, professional status, fiduciary authority or permission to trade.

## Legal and jurisdiction work

People may describe experience in legal research, contract and document operations, entity formation, title and registry processes, financial regulation, comparative law, arbitration, public policy or qualified legal practice.

Only an appropriately qualified person may represent themselves as providing legal practice. Jurisdiction and current professional status must be verified separately.

## Matching outcomes

M5isYOU produces pathways rather than a pass/fail result:

1. **Contribute now** — evidence aligns with bounded public work.
2. **Demonstrate** — an accessible work sample or conversation can establish the capability.
3. **Develop** — a learning, mentor or cohort pathway can develop it.

“No current match” is temporary, explainable and correctable. It must not become a permanent hidden label.

## Human and agent responsibilities

Approved agents may:

- extract proposed capabilities from a submitted page;
- translate the person's own language into the shared vocabulary;
- identify possible role families;
- explain which evidence supported a suggestion; and
- suggest learning or work-sample pathways.

The person must confirm extracted capabilities before they are used. Agents may not make final employment, compensation, grant, cohort-admission or governance decisions.

The [Fair Matching Standard](M5ISYOU-FAIR-MATCHING.md) defines prohibited inputs, explanations, human review, accommodations, correction and appeal.

## From self-description to portable evidence

M5isYOU separates the stages:

```text
self-described capability
→ person confirms the structured record
→ evidence is shared for a specific purpose
→ an accessible demonstration or review occurs
→ a qualified reviewer makes a limited attestation
→ a portable capability credential may be issued
```

Any credential must identify who verified what, using which evidence, for which purpose and for how long. It must not certify the person's entire worth, identity or future potential.

## Governance

Before operational use, M5isYOU requires:

- public review of the vocabulary and schemas;
- accessibility and disability-community review;
- documented selection purposes and job- or task-related criteria;
- validation and bias testing for each material use;
- a human review and accommodation process;
- data protection, retention, correction and deletion controls;
- monitoring for harmful screening effects; and
- qualified employment and data-protection counsel in each applicable jurisdiction.

The Foundation should publish versioned methodology, change records and aggregated evaluation results without exposing applicants or members.
