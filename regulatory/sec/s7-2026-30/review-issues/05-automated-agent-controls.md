# Review pre-activation controls for automated agents

Evaluate the recommendation that automated agents be subject to pre-activation controls covering accountable-principal identity, current credentials, jurisdiction, deterministic policy, required human or entity approval, revocation, and tamper-evident evidence.

Please provide public evidence, objections, implementation analysis, or proposed revisions. Do not submit confidential, privileged, identifying, investor, or nonpublic agency information.
