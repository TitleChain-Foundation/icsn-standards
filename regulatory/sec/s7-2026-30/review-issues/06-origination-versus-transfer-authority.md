# Review separation of origination and transfer authority

Evaluate the recommendation to separate authority to originate or register a digital representation from later authority to transfer, add or remove legends, correct records, or cancel the representation.

Please provide public evidence, objections, implementation analysis, or proposed revisions. Do not submit confidential, privileged, identifying, investor, or nonpublic agency information.
