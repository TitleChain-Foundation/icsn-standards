# Participate in ICSN Standards

The Foundation's immediate public priority is to build visibility, gather informed review and bring together contributors who can work across the full stack.

## Start with one action

1. **Star** `TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards` to show public support.
2. **Watch** the repository for releases, RFCs and discussions.
3. **Introduce yourself** in Discussions and name the area where you can help.
4. **Review or contribute** through an Issue, RFC, implementation, conformance test, documentation change or community translation.
5. **Consider organization membership** after sustained, constructive participation.

You do not need to understand every layer of the architecture before participating. The human-rooted foundation is that people should own and control their sovereign selves, while credentials validate limited claims or capabilities without making a platform the owner of the person.

## Areas where contributors are needed

### Open standards and public infrastructure

- open data models, schemas, portability and interoperability;
- conformance, testing, certification boundaries and reference implementations;
- Linux, open-source ecosystems, operating systems and edge infrastructure; and
- security, privacy, post-quantum readiness and resilient networking.

### Silicon, devices and software

- chip and silicon architecture;
- secure elements, hardware roots of trust and device manufacturing;
- handsets, edge devices and human-controlled interfaces; and
- protocol, application, SDK, tooling and systems engineering.

### Identity, credentials and risk

- human-rooted identity and identity lifecycle design;
- person-, organization-, agent- and asset-bound credentials, proof of humanity, selective disclosure and consent;
- identity, authority and credential requirements across account opening, contracting, asset issuance, custody, transfer, trading, settlement and recovery;
- fraud prevention, assurance, audit, market integrity, safety, cybersecurity and risk management;
- privacy-preserving compliance, sanctions and financial-crime controls that retain human agency and due process; and
- recovery, guardianship, accessibility and protection for vulnerable communities.

### Payments, finance and decentralized edge economics

- traditional finance and decentralized finance (DeFi), including payment systems, clearing, settlement, treasury, accounting, custody and financial infrastructure;
- Ricardian contracts that bind human-readable legal terms to machine-executable clauses, smart contracts and other forms of programmable agreement;
- digital assets and tokenized real-world assets, including their classification, issuance, title, provenance, custody, transfer, lifecycle, accounting and settlement;
- Bitcoin, other crypto-assets and network tokens, including interoperability with conventional banking, payment and market infrastructure;
- lawful markets and trading infrastructure for commodities, securities, notes, bonds, commercial paper, funds, derivatives and other financial or commercial instruments;
- decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), cooperatives and other digitally coordinated entities, including accountable authority, treasury controls and participant protections;
- community-governed pools and subsidiarity—placing decisions and resources at the most local capable level;
- M5 Money and decentralized edge-economy research;
- market data, price discovery, benchmarks, indexes and indices, CPI, GDP and new measures of productive or community value; and
- pathways from debt-led systems toward transparent, asset-driven economic models.

### Law, institutions and governance

- comparative law, jurisdiction, public policy and human rights;
- commercial, contract, property, corporate, securities, commodities, banking, payments, insolvency, tax and digital-asset law;
- legal and regulatory classification across traditional and crypto markets, including frameworks administered by bodies such as the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) and their counterparts in other jurisdictions;
- enforceability and evidence for Ricardian contracts, smart contracts, tokenized instruments, electronic signatures, records and cross-border transactions;
- financial institutions, exchanges, market intermediaries, cooperatives, collectives, DAOs and public-interest entities;
- accountable institutional and community governance, fiduciary controls, disclosure, market conduct, dispute resolution and adjudication models; and
- future cross-border, global and public-agency coordination architectures.

These areas describe an open research and standards agenda. They do not constitute legal, regulatory, tax or investment advice; an offer, solicitation or recommendation; the issuance or listing of an asset; or the operation of an exchange, broker, custodian, bank, clearinghouse or adjudicatory body. They do not claim that any named government, regulator, international organization, standards body, company or community has endorsed or joined the work. References to the SEC, CFTC or other authorities identify areas of law and regulatory research only. References to future global or adjudication architectures do not imply United Nations affiliation or authority.

## Optional M5BankOfMe pathway

M5 is one implementation and activation pathway; it is not a requirement for contributing to ICSN standards.

1. Review the public onboarding path at [m5bank.app](https://m5bank.app/).
2. Use the [M5 Build pathway](https://m5bank.app/m5-build.html) to join the IAM/M5BankOfMe waitlist when you are ready for the separate onboarding process.
3. Organizations and supporters may review the [Founding Cohort sponsor invitation](https://m5bank.app/m5-circle.html). Sponsorship helps fund developers, programmers and launch work; it does not purchase standards authority or conformance status.

Participants who establish a sustained, constructive contribution record may become eligible to apply for an announced contributor place in an M5Bank cohort series. Eligibility is not admission: account activation remains subject to a separate application, informed consent, capacity, program criteria and applicable onboarding requirements.

The Foundation may also announce mission grants or paid assignments for defined research, development, review, testing and community work. Membership or cohort status does not guarantee an award. Grants, compensation and any digital-asset payment must follow the separate [Cohort, Funding and Contributor Rewards Framework](COHORT-FUNDING-AND-CONTRIBUTOR-REWARDS.md).

M5 onboarding is consent-based and separate from GitHub participation. A GitHub handle is a service address, not a sovereign identity root. Repository activity must not be used to issue an account, credential, proof-of-humanity determination or governance authority without the required separate process and consent.

## Becoming an organization member

Organization membership is a trusted operational role, not the first participation step. Prospective members should normally establish a public record of useful, respectful work and demonstrate alignment with the Code of Conduct and governance process.

See [MEMBERSHIP-AND-ROLES.md](MEMBERSHIP-AND-ROLES.md) for the distinction between followers, participants, contributors, organization members, editors, maintainers and standards stewards.
