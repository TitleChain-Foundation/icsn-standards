# Public Infrastructure Commitment

## Identity at the root

TitleChain Foundation treats foundational sovereign identity as public-interest infrastructure, not as a subscription that a person must keep paying to remain themselves.

The program commitment is that the foundational **IAM identity-origin account is offered at no charge to the individual** and is designed to preserve that no-charge access over time.

That commitment applies to the foundational IAM account. Optional third-party services, compute, wallets, financial services, business services, premium tools, or other implementation layers may have their own separately disclosed terms and costs.

## What the IAM account is for

The IAM account is intended to establish a human-controlled origin for identity, provenance, consent, and access.

It is not intended to make TitleChain Foundation, M5, a sponsor, an employer, or an application the owner of the person.

The public activation model uses the sequence:

**I AM → I KNOW → I CAN → I DID → I CAN TEACH → I CAN EARN**

The sequence is designed to let a person build evidence of identity, learning, capability, work, teaching, and economic participation without reducing the person to a résumé, platform score, or sponsor-owned profile.

## Two live public starting actions: free IAM and the M5POD waitlist

The public pathway intentionally separates **foundational identity access** from
**reserving a place for future M5POD activation**.

### 1. Free IAM — `m5bank.app`

The foundational IAM identity-origin account is available at **[m5bank.app](https://m5bank.app/)**.

This is the no-charge identity starting point. A person may establish the foundational IAM without first enrolling in M5AgentClub, a cohort, a paid service, or another M5 program.

### 2. Reserve an M5POD place — `m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app`

The public waitlist is available at
**[m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app](https://m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app/)**.

The waitlist reserves a place in the pending activation pathway. It is not
controlled enrollment, cohort admission, M5BankofMe activation, credential
issuance, or activation of an M5POD.

The public waitlist is designed around member control:

- the member chooses what to submit;
- the member can leave information blank or mark it for private discussion;
- submitted information is not intended to be scraped, purchased, or inferred from unrelated third parties;
- member data is not intended for sale;
- the member may request correction, export, withdrawal, or deletion; and
- linked credentials validate only the claims they are issued to validate and do not grant ownership of the member's profile.

The distinction is deliberate:

```text
FREE IAM
m5bank.app
    ↓
M5POD WAITLIST
m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app
    ↓
future controlled enrollment + applicable member activation
```

No other public enrollment or activation URL is currently open. Implementation
details and privacy terms may evolve. The live service terms and privacy notice
control the operation of each service.

## GitHub participation never requires IAM

ICSN standards participation remains open and separate from account onboarding.

You do **not** need an IAM account to:

- read the specifications;
- open or comment on an issue;
- participate in a public Discussion;
- submit an RFC or pull request;
- review security, privacy, or interoperability work; or
- build an independent implementation.

A GitHub handle is not treated as a sovereign identity root. Repository activity must not be silently converted into an IAM account, credential, proof-of-humanity decision, or governance authority.

## IAM does not create standards authority

Creating an IAM account does not give a person a standards vote, Foundation office, certification, investment right, employment relationship, or preferential treatment.

Likewise, declining to create an IAM account does not reduce a person's ability to participate in the public standards process.

## Sponsoring access without owning the person

Sponsors may help underwrite the public infrastructure, open tooling, security, education, deployment capacity, and activation support required to make sovereign identity and build environments accessible.

A sponsor does not receive:

- ownership of the member or her identity;
- access to private member data merely because it funded access;
- advertising or profiling rights;
- standards or governance authority;
- exclusive access to cohort participants;
- a requirement that participants use the sponsor's cloud, model, wallet, bank, or product; or
- rights in the TitleChain Sovereign Purpose Trust.

GitHub Sponsors funding is directed to public open-source and public-interest work described in [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md). Separately documented cohort or program sponsorships may underwrite participant activation, education, compute, or implementation support, but those relationships remain subject to the same non-capture boundaries.

## First scaled activation: women-led ventures

The first major activation goal is to support **up to 100,000 women-led
ventures** beginning with the no-charge IAM identity origin at
**[m5bank.app](https://m5bank.app/)** and, by separate choice, reserving an
M5POD place through
**[m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app](https://m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app/)**.
Any later enrollment, M5BankofMe activation, capability proof, education,
agents, business infrastructure, or economic activity requires its own
announced process, consent, eligibility, capacity, and approval.

The goal is not to make 100,000 women dependent on M5. It is to demonstrate that a large population can begin with portable identity and build with a right to choose providers, tools, models, and implementation paths.

See [`initiatives/100K-WOMEN-LED-VENTURES.md`](initiatives/100K-WOMEN-LED-VENTURES.md).

## Public-interest test

The infrastructure should be judged by a simple test:

> Can a person establish her identity, prove what she can do, build, change providers, revoke delegated authority, export what is hers, and leave without surrendering ownership of herself?

If not, the implementation has not yet met the mission.
