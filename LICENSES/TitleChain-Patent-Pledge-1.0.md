# TitleChain Patent Pledge, Version 1.0 — Public Review Draft

> **Not in effect.** This is a public review draft. It takes effect only when the Trustee of the
> TitleChain Sovereign Purpose Trust adopts it and the Patent Owner signs it, and the signed,
> dated version is recorded in the Cyrus Commons Register. Until then it grants no rights.

## 1. Purpose

TitleChain Foundation exists so that everyone can build on open, human-first standards for
title, provenance, and accountable agents, and so that no company, sponsor, vendor, licensee,
or government can capture them.

Under this Pledge, the patents behind those standards are held for that purpose. Anyone may
implement the published standards without asking permission or paying a royalty, as long as
they do not use the standards to capture them or to attack the people who build on them.

## 2. Pledgors

- **Patent Owner:** Pamela Norton, owner of the Pledged Patents.
- **Trust:** the TitleChain Sovereign Purpose Trust, established by TitleChain Foundation, as
  exclusive licensee of the Pledged Patents.

Each Pledgor makes this Pledge for itself and binds its heirs, successors, assigns, and
exclusive licensees.

## 3. Definitions

**"Pledged Patents"** means:

1. U.S. Patent No. 11,720,888, *Decentralized Title Transfer and Validation of Assets*;
2. U.S. Patent No. 12,518,273, a continuation of 11,720,888, with the same title; and
3. every continuation, continuation-in-part, divisional, reissue, reexamination certificate, and
   foreign counterpart of either of them.

**"Published Standard"** means a specification, schema, RFC, or conformance criterion that
TitleChain Foundation publishes in this repository at Draft, Candidate, or Stable status, and
any Designated Method in Schedule A.

**"Covered Implementation"** means software, hardware, a service, or a system, to the extent it
implements a Published Standard, and that complies with the Cyrus Purpose Covenant (Section 7
of the Cyrus Purpose-Bound Constitutional Commons License 1.0), whether or not it uses any
Cyrus-licensed material.

**"Assertion"** means a lawsuit, administrative proceeding, or written demand alleging patent
infringement and seeking damages, a royalty, or an injunction.

## 4. The Pledge

Each Pledgor irrevocably promises, subject only to Section 6, not to make any Assertion under the
Pledged Patents against any person or entity for making, having made, using, selling, offering
for sale, importing, or distributing a Covered Implementation.

The Pledge is:

- **royalty-free** and requires no payment;
- **worldwide**;
- **automatic**: no registration, notice, or agreement is needed to rely on it; and
- **open to everyone**, including competitors of M5Capital Holdings LLC and implementers who
  never use M5.

## 5. What the Pledge does not cover

The Pledge does not license or cover:

1. anything beyond what is needed to implement a Published Standard;
2. the M5Canon rules engine, unpublished code, or trade secrets held by the Trust;
3. a trademark, certification mark, name, or logo, including TitleChain, M5Canon, CYRUS,
   M5, or any "Certified" mark (conformance claims and certification require separate written
   permission); or
4. any patent other than the Pledged Patents.

The Pledge does not narrow any patent license granted under another license. In particular,
code that a Pledgor contributes under the Apache License 2.0 carries that license's own patent
grant (Apache-2.0, Section 3), separately from this Pledge.

## 6. Ending the Pledge for a party

The Pledge ends as to a party, and its affiliates, if that party:

1. **attacks the commons**: makes an Assertion of any patent against a Pledgor, TitleChain
   Foundation, or any person or entity, based on a Covered Implementation; or
2. **captures the standards**: materially breaches the Cyrus Purpose Covenant, including by
   Capture as that license defines it, and does not cure the breach within a reasonable
   period after written notice, where the breach can be cured (Cyrus License Section 12).

The Pledge then ends only for that party. Everyone else keeps it, including the party's
customers and downstream users who remain compliant on their own. If an Assertion under
paragraph 1 is a counterclaim to a patent Assertion first brought against that party, the
Pledge does not end.

## 7. Durability

This Pledge is irrevocable except as Section 6 provides. It runs with the Pledged Patents.
Any transfer, assignment, or exclusive license of a Pledged Patent is subject to it. The Trust
may add Designated Methods to Schedule A. It may not remove any, or narrow this Pledge.

## 8. Disclaimer

The Pledge is a promise not to assert the Pledged Patents. It is not a warranty that a Covered
Implementation does not infringe anyone else's rights, and the Pledgors give no other warranty.

## 9. Contact

TitleChain Foundation. For licensing beyond this Pledge, contact the Foundation.
