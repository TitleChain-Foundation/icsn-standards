# Sovereign M5POD System Diagram — Accessible Description

> **Status: Public Review Draft.** This description accompanies the maintained Sovereign M5POD Mermaid source and its rendered formats.

The diagram has five primary areas.

The public commons contains ICSN open standards, M5Canon ontology and state rules, and TitleChain title and provenance evidence. These public functions provide standards and evidence without gaining custody of member-private data or value.

The human root contains IAM for human-rooted identity, credentials, recovery, and proof of life, followed by Bank of Me for the person's consent, rights, custody, authority, and delegation. Authority moves inward from this named human principal to the private M5POD boundary.

The private M5POD boundary contains M5Vault encrypted storage and recovery, M5POD personal operating data, the member-owned M5Brain private agent, M5Shell, M5App/Freedom Office, Obsidian, Matrix communications, WireGuard network paths, member-selected storage, and supported device platforms. Information enters agent context or leaves the boundary only under member authorization.

The sovereign systems intelligence area connects the M5 Canon Graph, M5Bank member ontology, and semantic reasoning for conformance, exposure analysis, and proof. Only privacy-filtered proofs, never private M5POD contents, cross into this review layer.

The legal and governance area separates Foundation and ICSN standards governance from operational legal functions. It states that public standards do not create custody, private-data access, or implied legal, regulatory, licensing, or endorsement authority. Operational legal actions remain credentialed, jurisdiction-aware, reviewable, and attributable to responsible humans or providers.

The diagram also places emerging non-human intelligence behind a human sovereignty gate. AI-native or embodied systems receive no blanket authority. Each deployment requires a named Bank of Me human principal, current proof of life and consent, ledger validation, and attested roles and skills.

The related activation document separately distinguishes scan-only review, place reservation, application, approval, IAM activation, M5POD pairing, operational status, suspension, revocation, withdrawal, deletion, and recovery. Viewing a demo, saving a place, submitting contact information, or authenticating to a website is not IAM or M5POD activation.
