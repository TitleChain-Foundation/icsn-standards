## What this Act does, in three sentences

Every Personal, Cooperative, and Small Business account gets a free, unmetered, private right to run open-weight AI — including on hardware they already own — with no new federal agency required. States administer and enforce it using commercial-law infrastructure most already have (UCC Article 12); Congress's role is to direct existing agencies (FTC, NIST) and enable interstate reciprocity, not build new bureaucracy. A dedicated section addresses the risk that frontier AI companies could end up defining safety, writing the compliance standard, and being shielded from antitrust scrutiny for coordinating on it — citing the companies' and legislators' own public proposals, not alleging anything unprovable.
