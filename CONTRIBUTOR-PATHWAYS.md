# Contribute by Skill

TitleChain Foundation currently has three GitHub organization members. You do
not need organization membership, IAM, an M5 account, or a funded role to make a
public contribution.

## One pathway

1. [Introduce yourself or share feedback](https://github.com/orgs/TitleChain-Foundation/discussions).
2. Choose a skill below and review a scoped Issue.
3. Comment on the Issue before starting substantial work.
4. Submit evidence, recommendations, documentation, tests, or a pull request.
5. A responsible reviewer evaluates the contribution before acceptance.

Public demo and onboarding feedback belongs in
[Questions & Help](https://github.com/orgs/TitleChain-Foundation/discussions/categories/questions-help).
Private identity, account, business, or security details must not be posted.

Issues marked [`good first issue`](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)
are designed for a bounded first contribution. Issues marked
[`help wanted`](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22)
need outside expertise.

Assignment to a maintainer identifies the responsible steward; it does not mean
the work is unavailable. Comment on the Issue to claim or coordinate a task.

## Boundaries

Participation does not create employment, compensation, organization membership,
an account, a credential, legal authority, or standards approval. Funded work is
offered only through a separate written process when resources are available.
