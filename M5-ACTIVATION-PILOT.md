# M5 Activation Pilot

> **Status:** Public Review Draft. This document describes an implementation and learning sequence; it is not itself an account, credential, payment service, business registration, employment offer, or guarantee.

```text
M5IAM
  ↓
TCID / M5HUM
  ↓
M5POD
  ↓
M5-CV / SOPHIA
  ↓
bounded M5AGT / operating role
  ↓
M5Canon Six Gates
  ↓
authorized agreement / title / registry / payment / business action
  ↓
evidence + reconciliation
```

The pilot should validate identity/recovery, private evidence controls, M5-CV/SOPHIA capability state, credential and revocation handling, bounded agent delegation, an approved workflow/state change, evidence/reconciliation, and a repeatable instructor/conformance package.

An agent may assist only inside the authority granted by its accountable M5HUM/legal principal. It cannot create a credential, extend an expired role, waive a restriction, or grant itself a capability.
