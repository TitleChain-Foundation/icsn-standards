# M5AGT Credentialed Operations

> **Status:** Public operating-policy reference.

**M5AGT** is the canonical type code for an autonomous/software agent operating under delegated authority. Every consequential M5AGT action must resolve to a named accountable **M5HUM** or lawful legal entity.

```text
M5HUM / legal principal
        ↓
current role + credential + jurisdiction
        ↓
explicit delegated capability
        ↓
M5Canon Six-Gate evaluation
        ↓
M5AGT action
        ↓
authorization/evidence receipt
```

## Hard limits

An M5AGT may not:

- grant itself authority;
- exceed the current authority of its principal;
- continue using an expired/revoked role or credential;
- override jurisdictional restrictions;
- bypass required human/entity approval;
- independently create legal, regulatory, professional, or governance status; or
- alter an authoritative ownership/registry record outside the scope of the actor legally responsible for that record.

## Required delegation fields

Operational credentials/delegations should identify the accountable principal, role, purpose, systems/data, permitted actions, prohibited actions, asset/entity scope, jurisdiction, approval thresholds, effective/expiry times, revocation process, policy version, and audit requirements.

## Public standards use

M5AGTs may assist standards contributors with routing, drafting, testing, documentation, and conformance work, but they do not independently adopt or certify an ICSN standard.
