# Cyrus Commons designation process

No material enters the Cyrus Constitutional Commons merely because it is
publicly visible. Before designation, the accountable reviewer must confirm:

- the Foundation or contributor owns the material or has authority to release
  it;
- the material contains no credentials, keys, secrets, private records,
  biometric data, confidential contracts, restricted security information, or
  incompatible third-party content;
- purpose-bound public and commercial reuse is intentional;
- no trademark, patent, hosted-service, financial-product, certification,
  credential, private-system, or private-data right is accidentally granted;
- the exact path, version, date, authority, status, and content hash are entered
  in the Cyrus Commons Register; and
- the file carries the identifier `LicenseRef-CYRUS-PCCL-1.0` or inherits it
  from an expressly designated directory notice.

Designations and status changes must be public, dated, attributable, and
reviewable. Register states may include `public-review`, `active`,
`superseded`, `suspended`, `deprecated`, or `withdrawn`.
