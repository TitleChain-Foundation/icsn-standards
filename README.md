# ICSN Standards

Open specifications, RFCs and conformance work for human-rooted identity and sovereign networks.

This repository is the public standards workspace of the [TitleChain Foundation](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation). It supports interoperable systems for people, sole proprietors, startups, new ventures, cooperatives, institutions, governments and sovereign nation states.

## What we are building

ICSN explores a neutral, human-rooted public coordination layer across the full technology and institutional stack—from silicon and devices to identity, payments, law and governance.

The public idea is simple: each person should own and control their sovereign self, while credentials can validate specific claims, capabilities and proof-of-humanity signals without transferring ownership of the person to a platform or institution.

We are seeking contributors in:

- open data standards, interoperability, conformance and open-source governance;
- Linux, operating systems and edge infrastructure;
- chip, silicon, secure hardware, handset and device development;
- identity, credentials, privacy, security and proof-of-humanity methods;
- software, protocols, developer tooling and reference implementations;
- payments, financial infrastructure, institutions and risk management;
- law, public policy, jurisdiction, rights and cross-border coordination;
- subsidiarity and community-governed economic or payment pools;
- decentralized edge economics and M5 Money research; and
- economic measurement, including indexes and indices, CPI, GDP, asset valuation and pathways from debt-led systems toward asset-driven models.

The work may also inform future global adjudication, cooperative governance and public-agency architectures. These are research and standards directions; they do not imply affiliation with, endorsement by or authority from the United Nations or any other institution.

## Standards process

Proposals advance through **Draft**, **Candidate**, **Stable**, and **Deprecated** stages under [open governance](GOVERNANCE.md). Material standards changes require public review, documented decisions, security and privacy analysis, conformance criteria, and implementation evidence.

Publication in this repository does not by itself make a proposal a Foundation standard.

## Credentialed agent operations

[M5Agent Credentialed Operations](M5AGENT_OPERATIONS.md) defines the proposed OSHINA contribution-steward role, scoped authority levels, credential requirements, human accountability and privacy limits. Named agents are not represented as deployed or authorized until their credentials and accountable stewards are established.

## Optional M5 implementation and onboarding pathway

ICSN standards are implementation-neutral. M5 is one implementation and activation pathway helping validate the architecture in practice.

- [Review the M5Bank pathway](https://m5bank.app/)
- [Join the M5 Build pathway and IAM waitlist](https://m5bank.app/m5-build.html)
- [Explore cohort sponsorship](https://m5bank.app/m5-circle.html)
- [View the activation initiative](https://m5activate.netlify.app/)

GitHub participation and M5 onboarding are separate. Starring, watching, discussing, contributing to or joining this repository does not automatically create an M5BankOfMe account, identity credential, proof-of-humanity status, governance authority or legal relationship. M5 onboarding is a separate, consent-based process.
