# Founding Roles and Activation Pathway

> **Status: public role map and invitation to contribute.** This document describes capabilities the TitleChain Foundation expects to activate as cohorts, registries, member accounts, M5Native workflows and market-facing products develop. It is not a representation that every role is currently funded, employed or open for immediate appointment.

## Why these roles exist

The Foundation is bringing together standards contributors, operators and domain specialists who can work through documented workflows with accountable internal agents, validate those workflows in real cohort and registry settings, and help turn repeatable results into safe, interoperable products for the wider market.

The work spans:

- cohort formation, member support and interactive learning;
- sovereign identity, IAM and M5Bank account activation;
- entity, asset and jurisdiction registries;
- M5Native agent workflows and sovereign knowledge systems;
- personal data stores, wallets, payments and settlement;
- title, commercial instruments and real-world asset infrastructure;
- economic measurement, M5Global indices and market data; and
- governance, assurance, arbitration and cross-border productization.

Standards participation remains open and implementation-neutral. Appointment to an operating role, access to private implementation systems, an M5Bank account, compensation and governance authority each require separate authorization.

## How people and agents work together

Each operating role is expected to use approved internal agents for bounded research, intake, drafting, validation, testing, documentation and workflow support. Agents do not replace accountable people or institutions.

Every consequential workflow should identify:

- the responsible human or institution;
- the agent and tools being used;
- the data and authority available to them;
- required reviews and approvals;
- evidence, provenance and checkpoints;
- conflicts and separation-of-duty requirements; and
- the conditions for publishing or productizing the result.

Private keys, personal identity records, member financial data, confidential implementation code and production controls do not belong in this public repository.

## Activation waves

The waves describe operating dependencies, not rank or personal importance. Some roles may begin as fractional, advisory, contributor or working-group responsibilities before becoming staffed positions.

### Wave 0 — Canon, authority and operating controls

#### Chief Canon and Sovereign Systems Architect

Protects the coherence of M5Canon, the sovereign-purpose architecture and the full M5 stack. This role maintains architectural decision records, prevents unreviewed divergence and technical debt, and ensures that new code, workflows and products remain aligned with human sovereignty, purpose limitation and the adopted governance model.

#### Chief Activation and Integration Officer

Owns the cross-functional operating map from cohort intake through identity, registry, accounts, learning, support and product activation. This person coordinates dependencies, service ownership, readiness gates, incident escalation and launch sequencing without absorbing the independent authority of legal, security, market or governance functions.

#### General Counsel and Global Regulatory Architecture Lead

Coordinates qualified counsel across entity formation, registries, financial instruments, securities and commodities, payments, privacy, intellectual property, sanctions, tax, arbitration and participating jurisdictions. Counsel determines where specialist local advice, registration, licensing or regulatory engagement is required.

#### Chief Security, Privacy and Applied Cryptography Officer

Owns threat modeling, key and wallet security, cryptographic design review, privacy engineering, identity assurance, incident response, secure communications and agent/tool security. This function is independent of delivery pressure and may stop unsafe releases.

#### Foundation Finance, Treasury and Grants Lead

Establishes budgeting, accounting, treasury controls, grant administration, cohort sponsorship records, procurement, tax coordination and contributor-payment operations. No person approves their own award or payment.

#### Independent Risk, Audit and Controls Lead

Maps workflows to risks and controls, tests whether controls operate in practice, preserves audit evidence and reports material exceptions independently. This includes technology, financial, registry, agent, market and governance controls.

### Wave 1 — Cohorts, identity and registry activation

#### Cohort Activation and Member Success Lead

Designs the end-to-end member journey from waitlist and consent through IAM, M5BankOfMe, BankOfMe and eligible business/entity pathways. This role owns service standards, escalation, accessibility and the feedback loop between members and product teams.

#### Learning, Curriculum and 100K Ventures Program Lead

Develops the repeatable learning and venture-support model for the initial women-led business cohorts. The role coordinates teachers and learning partners, interactive curricula, trained member agents, milestone evidence and templates that can be localized without erasing community context.

#### Global Registry and Jurisdiction Activation Lead

Scales the registry workflow for nation, state, tribal, territorial, municipal and other governance-chain contexts. This role coordinates chain reservations, authority verification, jurisdiction activation, entity formation pathways, Secretary of State or equivalent filings, templates, agreements and registry operations.

Reservation or technical deployment of a chain does not itself establish governmental affiliation, sovereign authority or legal recognition.

#### Identity, Credentials and Trust Architect

Owns IAM origin and provenance, identity lifecycle, proof-of-control and proof-of-humanity methods, credentials, selective disclosure, recovery, guardianship and assurance levels. The architecture must preserve context separation and must not treat a person as property of a platform or institution.

#### Legal Identity Continuity and swiftBRIDGE Lead

Coordinates verification and tokenized representation of legacy legal, institutional and digital presence, then links that evidence to newly issued or recognized M5 identities and accounts. The work includes DNS and ENS mappings, institutional identifiers, continuity relationships, provenance, status and revocation.

#### Product, Service Design and Accessibility Lead

Turns complex identity, registry, account and learning processes into understandable member experiences across desktop, mobile and assisted channels. This includes inclusive research, multilingual design, accessibility, consent comprehension and protection for vulnerable communities.

#### Community Governance and Contributor Stewardship Lead

Builds healthy public participation, Discussions, moderation, contributor development, working groups and transparent decision records. This role coordinates human oversight of contribution-support agents while keeping public contribution separate from employment, account eligibility and standards authority.

### Wave 2 — Sovereign agent and member platform

#### M5Native Agent Workflow Architect

Establishes the reusable workflow system through which credentialed M5Native agents perform bounded work. The role owns routing, specialist-agent delegation, human approval, tool contracts, checkpoints, evaluation, observability and portable framework adapters.

#### Founding Architect, Sovereign Knowledge, Memory and Agent Graphs

Designs the linked knowledge, authority, workflow and evidence graphs supporting a person's private context. This role establishes memory classes, ontology, provenance, context isolation, retrieval controls, corrections, revocation, portability and connections among Obsidian, Graphity, M5POD and authorized agents.

#### M5POD and Sovereign Shell Platform Lead

Scales the member-controlled Solid/POD and sovereign-shell experience across Linux, Windows, macOS, mobile and tablet environments. The role coordinates local-first data, synchronization, recovery, Freedom Office services, open-weight models and optional member-authorized paid model connections.

#### Identity and Member Platform Engineers

Build the front-end, mobile, full-stack and platform services required for IAM, account activation, credentials, consent, recovery and member support. Engineers work from shared contracts and conformance tests rather than creating incompatible product-specific identity models.

#### Payments, Wallets and Settlement Lead

Owns wallet and payment architecture, asset and money account types, treasury flows, community-governed pools, settlement, reconciliation and recovery. The role coordinates traditional and digital rails while preserving legal title, account identity and separation of duties.

#### Developer Platform and M5OpenAPI Marketplace Lead

Creates stable APIs, SDKs, schemas, sandboxes, documentation, partner onboarding and the pathway for approved workflows to become reusable services. Marketplace publication requires security, licensing, authority, privacy and commercial review.

#### Platform Reliability and Release Engineering Lead

Owns environments, deployment, observability, backups, disaster recovery, capacity, incident management, dependency integrity and reproducible releases. This role establishes service objectives and ensures that long-running agent workflows and registry operations resume safely after failure.

#### Conformance, Agent Evaluation and Quality Lead

Develops reference cases, protocol conformance suites, workflow simulations, model and agent evaluations, adversarial tests and release evidence. This function verifies both successful outcomes and correct refusal, escalation, recovery and privacy behavior.

#### Data Governance and Privacy Steward

Defines classification, consent, retention, minimization, lineage, access, deletion, member-data aggregation and acceptable secondary use. This steward reviews data products and prevents operational convenience from silently becoming authorization.

#### Standards Editor and Technical Documentation Lead

Turns implementation learning into legible specifications, RFCs, schemas, diagrams, operator guides and public review material. This role maintains vocabulary consistency and a verifiable boundary between adopted standards, public drafts, research directions and private implementation claims.

### Wave 3 — Markets, indices and productization

#### Founding Head of Global Markets, Title and Jurisdiction Infrastructure

Takes the implemented M5 title, instrument and jurisdiction workflows through technical validation, legal structuring, institutional pilots and production scale. The remit includes commercial paper, notes, bonds, receivables, titled real-world and digital assets, transfer, custody, collateral, settlement, insurance and cross-border operations.

This leader understands the full lifecycle but does not combine issuer, trader, valuer, insurer, registry, arbitrator and compliance authority in one function.

#### Founding Chief Economist and Head of M5Global Index Methodology

Develops the economic and statistical framework behind the M5Global index family, M5 Money research and member-centered edge-economy measurement. The work combines verified external markets and real-world assets with permissioned, privacy-preserving aggregated observations from M5Bank members.

Initial research areas include real-world asset prices, member cost and purchasing power, productive assets, edge-economy activity, liquidity, resilience and jurisdiction-level measures. Methodologies, revisions, source quality and conflicts must be transparent.

#### Index Administration and Benchmark Oversight Lead

Operates index calculation, methodology governance, data-contributor controls, complaints, restatements, continuity and independent oversight. This function remains separate from trading and from issuers of products that reference an index.

#### Real-World Asset Data, Valuation and Oracle Lead

Builds the taxonomy, data lineage and price-quality system for physical, financial and digital assets. The role distinguishes completed transactions, executable quotes, appraisals, comparable estimates, model values and member observations, with confidence and staleness controls.

#### Exchange Operations and Market Integrity Lead

Designs participant admission, order and execution controls, surveillance, conflicts management, market-abuse detection, incident handling and fair-access rules for any M5Global market venue. The title “exchange” remains subject to the legal classification and authorization required in each jurisdiction.

#### Insurance, Bonding and Actuarial Risk Lead

Designs independent underwriting pathways for insurance, surety bonds, guarantees, reserves and other risk-transfer or credit-enhancement structures. This function defines covered events and claims evidence and does not treat tokenization as proof of insurability.

#### Institutional and Public-Sector Activation Lead

Develops verified relationships with governments, tribes, agencies, registries, banks, insurers, market operators, educators, standards bodies and implementation partners. Public descriptions must distinguish outreach, pilot participation, signed agreement, delegated authority and formal endorsement.

#### Arbitration and Cross-Border Enforcement Lead

Coordinates qualified arbitration specialists to operationalize a New York Convention-aligned process for eligible disputes. This includes express consent, seat and governing law, tribunal formation, notice, evidence, due process, signed awards and court-ready records. It does not imply United Nations or governmental affiliation.

#### Commercial Product and Partnership Lead

Converts validated internal workflows into clearly scoped products, service agreements, pricing, support models and partner implementations. Productization begins only after authority, security, data, legal, licensing, conformance and operating-readiness gates are satisfied.

## Independent functions and conflicts

Certain functions must remain independent even when a small launch team requires people to wear more than one operational hat. Conflicts must be disclosed, reviewed and mitigated.

The following decisions should not be controlled by the same interested party:

- instrument issuance and independent title registration;
- trading and benchmark administration;
- asset contribution and independent valuation;
- underwriting and approval of one's own claim;
- grant application and award approval;
- registry application and final authority verification;
- product delivery and independent security or control sign-off; and
- a party to a dispute and selection or direction of the deciding tribunal.

## Shared operating expectations

People serving in these roles should be able to:

- work across disciplines without claiming expertise or authority they do not possess;
- translate private implementation learning into appropriately scoped public standards work;
- document decisions, assumptions, evidence and unresolved risks;
- supervise agent-assisted work and verify material outputs;
- design for privacy, security, accessibility and international use;
- disclose conflicts and respect independent review;
- distinguish prototypes from production-ready or legally authorized services; and
- leave systems more modular, testable and understandable than they found them.

## Ways to begin

People do not need to wait for a formal title to contribute to public standards work.

1. Star and watch the repository.
2. Read [PARTICIPATE.md](PARTICIPATE.md) and the repository policies.
3. Introduce yourself in [Discussions](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/discussions) with the role area or working group you can support.
4. Identify a bounded public contribution: research, RFC review, threat model, methodology critique, test, schema, documentation or implementation evidence.
5. Build a constructive contribution record.
6. Apply through a separately announced cohort, grant, advisory, contractor or operating-role pathway when available.

See [MEMBERSHIP-AND-ROLES.md](MEMBERSHIP-AND-ROLES.md) for the distinction among public participation, GitHub organization membership and authorized operating roles. See [COHORT-FUNDING-AND-CONTRIBUTOR-REWARDS.md](COHORT-FUNDING-AND-CONTRIBUTOR-REWARDS.md) for the proposed funding and compensation framework.

## Important boundaries

- This role map is not an offer of employment, compensation, tokens, grants or governance authority.
- Publication of a role does not mean it has been filled or funded.
- Implementation work in a private repository is not automatically a public standard, audited system, legally effective instrument or production-ready service.
- Names corresponding to governments, nations, tribes, agencies or jurisdictions do not establish affiliation, delegated authority or recognition.
- References to the New York Convention describe a design objective for eligible arbitration workflows and do not imply United Nations or UNCITRAL endorsement.
- Financial, legal, tax, regulatory, actuarial and arbitration work requires appropriately qualified professionals in the relevant jurisdictions.

The Foundation expects this map to evolve through public review, implementation learning and recorded governance decisions.
