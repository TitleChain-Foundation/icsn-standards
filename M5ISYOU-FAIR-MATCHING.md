# M5isYOU Fair Matching Standard

> **Status: Public Review Draft for the people's review.** This proposed standard governs capability suggestions made from M5isYOU profiles. It does not authorize automated employment decisions.

## 1. Human purpose

M5isYOU exists to reveal contribution, demonstration and learning pathways that conventional résumé screening may miss. It must not recreate an applicant-tracking system under a different name.

## 2. No invisible score

The system must not produce or expose a single candidate score, ranking, employability rating, personality score, culture-fit score or permanent suitability label.

It may produce a role-specific explanation containing:

- capabilities supported by confirmed evidence;
- capabilities that remain unverified;
- capabilities that may be developed;
- the exact role requirements considered;
- the next available human, demonstration or learning step; and
- the profile version and matching-policy version used.

## 3. Prohibited inputs and inferences

Matching must not use or infer:

- race, color, ethnicity, caste or national origin;
- sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation or pregnancy;
- age or date of birth;
- religion or belief;
- disability, health, medical, genetic or biometric information;
- facial expression, voice emotion, accent or appearance analysis;
- marital, family or caregiving status;
- residential address or neighborhood as a proxy for status;
- credit history, prior salary or personal financial condition;
- protected leave, employment gaps or unemployment duration;
- school prestige, social network status or follower counts;
- private social-media surveillance;
- personality or psychological predictions;
- data obtained without an authorized matching purpose; or
- similarity to historically successful employees as a substitute for job-related evidence.

Sensitive information collected for lawful monitoring or accommodation must be segregated, access-controlled and unavailable to the matching process.

## 4. Person confirmation

An agent may propose structured capabilities extracted from a page, but the person must be able to:

- see the proposal in plain language;
- accept, edit or remove every capability;
- correct evidence attribution;
- add language missing from the vocabulary; and
- refuse automated extraction without losing access to an equivalent pathway.

Unconfirmed inferred capabilities must not be used.

## 5. Job- and task-related role maps

Every role map must identify outcomes, essential capabilities, developable capabilities, acceptable evidence and prohibited inputs. Requirements must be connected to the actual work rather than a generalized concept of professionalism or cultural fit.

The same verified role map and policy version must be used consistently for similarly situated participants. Any exception requires a recorded reason and review.

## 6. Three pathways

The system may suggest:

1. **Contribute now** for bounded work supported by evidence.
2. **Demonstrate** through an accessible work sample or structured conversation.
3. **Develop** through learning, mentoring or a cohort.

A fourth state, **no current pathway**, must include an explanation, correction process and date for reconsideration. It must not silently prevent future opportunities.

## 7. Human review

An accountable person or authorized panel makes consequential decisions involving:

- cohort admission;
- employment or contractor selection;
- grant or compensation awards;
- credentials or verified capability status;
- access to private systems;
- governance authority; and
- rejection from a specifically applied-for opportunity.

Reviewers receive the capability evidence needed for the decision, not unrelated profile information.

## 8. Accessible alternatives and accommodations

People must be told:

- what technology is being used;
- what capabilities are being evaluated;
- how to request an accommodation;
- how to use a non-automated alternative; and
- that requesting an accommodation will not reduce their opportunity.

Work samples must measure the capability required by the work rather than unrelated sensory, motor, communication or time-pressure characteristics.

Accommodation information remains outside the matching record.

## 9. Evidence and verification

Self-description, submitted evidence, reviewer attestation and verified credential are distinct states.

The system must not:

- convert a self-description into a verified claim;
- invent experience or credentials;
- treat a link as proof of authorship;
- publish private evidence;
- treat a credential as proof of unrelated capability; or
- retain evidence beyond its authorized purpose and period.

## 10. Explanation, correction and appeal

For every material suggestion or decision, the person must be able to obtain:

- the role map used;
- the capabilities and evidence considered;
- the reason for the result;
- the name or function of the accountable reviewer;
- instructions for correcting the profile;
- an accessible review or appeal channel; and
- the retention and deletion schedule.

An appeal must be reviewed by a person who did not make the original disputed decision where practical.

## 11. Privacy and data lifecycle

Profiles are private by default. Collection and use must be purpose-specific, minimal, time-limited and revocable where applicable.

The system must support:

- field-level sharing choices;
- approved-audience controls;
- expiration;
- access history;
- correction;
- portable export;
- deletion or legally required restriction; and
- separation between public contribution and employment or financial decisions.

Profiles and evidence must not be used to train an external model without separate informed authorization.

## 12. Testing and accountability

Before a material use, the responsible operator must document:

- the purpose and affected people;
- the role-related validity of each criterion;
- accessibility testing;
- privacy and security review;
- harmful-bias and disparate-screening analysis;
- false-positive and false-negative behavior;
- human oversight and escalation;
- incident and complaint handling; and
- a stop-use threshold.

Testing continues after deployment. A vendor statement or general model evaluation is not sufficient evidence that a particular M5isYOU use is fair or lawful.

## 13. Public reporting

The responsible operator should publish, without exposing individuals:

- current methodology and version history;
- material changes;
- role maps in use;
- evaluation scope and limitations;
- aggregate correction and appeal information;
- known incidents and remediations where safe; and
- the accountable governance contact.

## 14. Professional and jurisdictional review

Employment, contractor, education, grant, privacy and accessibility requirements differ across jurisdictions. Operational use requires qualified review for the specific program and location.

Following this draft does not by itself establish legal compliance.
