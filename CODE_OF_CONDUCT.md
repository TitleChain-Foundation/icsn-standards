# Code of Conduct

The TitleChain Foundation is committed to a respectful, safe and globally inclusive standards community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people, communities and sovereign perspectives with dignity.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Make room for participants with different languages, abilities, resources and levels of technical experience.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, consent, cultural knowledge and community authority.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, or misuse of community information are not permitted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository activity, Discussions, Foundation events and other spaces represented as official TitleChain Foundation community venues.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Foundation’s maintainers through GitHub. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an Issue or Discussion. Maintainers may remove content, limit interaction, temporarily suspend participation or permanently exclude participants when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, handled consistently and avoid conflicts of interest. This community policy is not a substitute for applicable law or the Foundation’s formal corporate governance documents.
