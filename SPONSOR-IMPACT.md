# Sponsor Impact

This page records public outcomes supported through the active TitleChain Foundation [GitHub Sponsors program](https://github.com/sponsors/TitleChain-Foundation).

## Reporting principles

- Report shipped public work, not promotional projections.
- Link to issues, pull requests, releases, specifications, tests, or other verifiable artifacts.
- Respect anonymous support and sponsor visibility choices.
- Use aggregate reporting where individual attribution is inappropriate.
- Keep sponsorship separate from standards authority, conformance decisions, and private participant data.

## Impact-note template

### YYYY-MM — Short outcome title

- **Workstream:** Standards | Security | Reference implementation | Infrastructure | Release | General open source
- **Outcome:** What was completed and made public.
- **Evidence:** Links to the relevant issue, pull request, release, or artifact.
- **Support:** Named sponsor with permission, “anonymous sponsor,” or aggregate supporter group.
- **Status:** Delivered | In progress | Reallocated
- **Notes:** Material limitations, changes, or follow-up work.
