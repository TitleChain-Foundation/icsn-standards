# SOPHIA 37-Track Participation Map

> **Status: Public Review Navigation Map.** This page groups the 37 canonical tracks in the SOPHIA Public Role Registry so participants can find relevant work. The eight families are navigation aids, not new credentials, departments, employment categories, or changes to the canonical track names.

The canonical [SOPHIA public role catalog](../roles/catalog/sophia-public-role-catalog.json) contains **636 public-review role records across 37 tracks**. SOPHIA is a discovery, capability, curriculum, and role vocabulary. Inclusion does not mean that a role is open, funded, appointed, licensed, credentialed, or approved.

## Use the map

When introducing yourself or a proposal, identify:

1. **Participation family** — the broad navigation group above.
2. **Canonical SOPHIA track** — use the exact public catalog name when one fits.
3. **Contribution mode** — infrastructure/provider, economic data/methodology, product/use case, workforce/capability, training/curriculum, or independent assurance/governance.
4. **Relationship to the role** — perform today, learning toward, ready to demonstrate, able to teach, able to mentor/review, or able to offer a service.
5. **Public-safe evidence** — optional work samples, publications, code, methods, or credentials that the holder is authorized and chooses to disclose.
6. **Next bounded contribution** — one review, mapping, test, dataset description, methodology, curriculum unit, issue, or pull request.

Start in [Discussion 22 — Providers, Economic Data, Workforce & Training](https://github.com/orgs/TitleChain-Foundation/discussions/22). Follow the privacy, status, evidence, licensing, and non-endorsement boundaries in [Build the Public Economic Data Commons](../COMMONS-CONTRIBUTION-TRACKS.md).

To see how holder-selected capability information could connect to an optional activation journey, [explore the M5POD-to-M5-CV demo](https://m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app/demo). To reserve a place and proceed toward sharing skills in M5-CV, [join the M5POD waitlist](https://m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app/). The demo and waitlist are optional M5 implementation pathways and are not required for public standards participation.

The taxonomy should follow people and the changing economy. People do not exist to fit the taxonomy, and participants may propose roles or tracks that are not yet represented.
