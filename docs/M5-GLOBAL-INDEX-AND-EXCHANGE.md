# M5 Global Index and Exchange

> **Status:** Public economic-intelligence architecture.

**M5 Global Index and Exchange** is the public index/data-exchange layer of M5Global. It is intended to let agencies, economists, statisticians, researchers, standards communities, asset-sector authorities, and public-interest technologists inspect and build upon privacy-preserving, provenance-aware economic indicators and methodologies.

“Exchange” here means exchange of economic data, index methodologies, revision histories, signals, and machine-readable public intelligence. It does **not** mean a securities exchange, ATS, broker-dealer, transfer agent, clearing agency, custodian, or trading venue.

```text
TitleChain provenance
+ authorized M5 Ledger evidence
+ jurisdiction / sector / asset state
        ↓
privacy + aggregation controls
        ↓
M5 GLOBAL INDEX AND EXCHANGE
        ↓
M5GPI / GGP / jurisdiction / sector / asset indicators
        ↓
agencies + statisticians + economists + researchers
```

## Indicator families

The architecture may support the **M5 Global Producer Index (M5GPI)**, **Gross Global Production (GGP)**, and other jurisdiction/sector/asset/production/utilization indicators.

Every published indicator family should expose methodology version, source lineage, coverage, aggregation thresholds, privacy method, valuation method where relevant, revision policy, uncertainty/confidence where appropriate, conflicts, governance, and reproducibility.

Publication as an M5 indicator does not make an output an official government statistic or regulated benchmark. Named professional organizations and agencies are intended stakeholder/reviewer communities unless a specific adoption or partnership is separately documented.
