# Sophia Activation Priorities — Roles, Skills, and Edge Economics

> **Status: Activation reference supporting the Sophia Public Role Registry.**

## Existing public registry

The existing [Sophia Public Role Registry](../SOPHIA-PUBLIC-ROLE-REGISTRY.md) is the TitleChain Foundation's public-review vocabulary for existing, emerging, and experimental work.

The current public registry documents:

- **636 role records**
- **37 tracks**

It is a discovery and curriculum taxonomy, not a claim that 636 funded positions are currently open.

## Demand-led activation

The full role map can remain broad while activation is prioritized around skills and capabilities in greatest demonstrated demand across:

- local communities;
- small businesses;
- emerging industries;
- AI-native businesses;
- critical infrastructure;
- public institutions;
- the M5 ecosystem; and
- the broader producer economy.

```text
ECONOMIC / COMMUNITY NEED
          ↓
Skills in Greatest Demand
          ↓
Sophia Role + Capability Mapping
          ↓
Learning / Demonstration Pathway
          ↓
M5POD Member
          ↓
Real Contribution
          ↓
Evidence
          ↓
M5-CV
          ↓
New Economic Opportunity
```

Demand determines which pathways are prioritized first. It does not determine a person's worth or restrict what they are allowed to become.

## Sophia + M5POD + M5-CV

```text
SOPHIA
role + capability vocabulary
        ↓
M5POD
member-controlled identity and learning environment
        ↓
LEARN / DEMONSTRATE / CONTRIBUTE
        ↓
evidence generated through real activity
        ↓
M5-CV
portable capability and contribution record
```

A member can use a Sophia role as:

- a role they perform today;
- a role supported by evidence or a credential;
- a role they are learning toward;
- a role they are ready to demonstrate;
- a role they can teach;
- a role they can mentor or review; or
- a role under which they can offer services.

People must remain free to propose new roles and capabilities.

> **The taxonomy follows people and the changing economy. People do not exist to fit the taxonomy.**
