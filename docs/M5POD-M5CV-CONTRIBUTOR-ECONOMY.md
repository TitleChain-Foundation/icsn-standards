# M5POD, M5-CV, and the Contributor Economy

> **Status: M5 implementation and activation reference.**

## Purpose

An activated M5POD is intended to become more than a technical account. It is the human-controlled foundation from which a person can build a continuously evolving record of capability, contribution, learning, evidence, and economic participation.

That record is reflected through the member-controlled **M5-CV**.

## Contribution becomes portable evidence

Meaningful work can become evidence in a member's M5-CV across software, design, statistics, economics, accounting, recruiting, education, operations, security, research, policy, communications, community work, and new roles that emerge over time.

The system should not treat prior title, degree, employer, résumé, social following, or years in a conventional career path as a measure of human worth.

The useful questions are:

- What can this person contribute?
- What have they demonstrated?
- What are they learning?
- What impact have they made?
- What could they become capable of next?

```text
I AM
  ↓
I WANT TO LEARN
  ↓
I CAN
  ↓
I CONTRIBUTED
  ↓
I DID
  ↓
I CAN PROVE IT
  ↓
I CAN TEACH
  ↓
I CAN EARN
  ↓
I CAN HELP BUILD WHAT COMES NEXT
```

## Evidence, not an invisible score

M5 should not reduce people to one automated ranking or permanent internal score.

The goal is **portable, explainable evidence** that may include:

- project/workstream;
- role;
- demonstrated capability;
- contribution;
- work evidence;
- authorized attestations;
- credentials;
- skills developed;
- production outcomes;
- teaching/mentoring;
- member-authorized economic outcomes; and
- provenance explaining how the claim was established.

The member controls the underlying M5-CV and disclosure of their evidence.

## Open to the person — focused on capability

M5POD activation in the commons is intended to be open to people from different backgrounds, disciplines, experiences, communities, and ways of describing themselves.

The system should not determine a person's value based on how they present themselves.

The operating questions are:

- What do you want to learn?
- What do you want to build or contribute?
- What skills do you already have?
- What evidence would help you demonstrate them?
- What pathway can help you move forward?

Legitimate security, legal, credential, professional-license, or role requirements may apply where necessary for a particular activity.

## Contributor flywheel

```text
person activates M5POD
        ↓
identifies existing capabilities
        ↓
learns new skills
        ↓
contributes to real work
        ↓
creates attributable evidence
        ↓
M5-CV grows
        ↓
new roles and opportunities become possible
        ↓
person earns / builds / teaches
        ↓
helps activate the next contributors
        ↓
the network gains capability
```

The commons should not merely consume volunteer labor. Where appropriate and authorized, contribution should help the individual build:

**capability + evidence + reputation + relationships + economic opportunity + ownership of their own work history**

while strengthening shared infrastructure.

## Edge economics

```text
HUMAN CAPABILITY
        ↓
LOCAL PRODUCTION
        ↓
COMMUNITY ECONOMY
        ↓
REGIONAL NETWORKS
        ↓
GLOBAL OPEN TRADE
```

The objective is strong local economic capacity that remains globally interoperable.

> **Do not ask people to prove that they already fit the old economy. Help them discover, build, demonstrate, and own the capabilities they need for the next one.**
>
> **The commons should create stronger humans, not merely more software.**
