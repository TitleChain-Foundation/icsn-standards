**ICSN — Internet Cooperative for Sovereign Networks** is an open standards initiative for interoperable identity, credentials, authority, title and asset records, privacy-preserving data systems, accountable agents, and jurisdiction-aware network infrastructure.

The TitleChain Foundation stewards the public standards process. ICSN materials are intended to be publicly reviewable and independently implementable, subject to applicable licenses, contribution terms, trademark rules, and governance procedures.

## M5 and ICSN

ICSN is a public standards and governance framework.

M5, M5Bank, TitleChain, M5Wallet, M5POD, M5Vault, M5 Agents, and M4–M5 provider pathways are implementation concepts, participation profiles, or independently operated service environments. No implementation, sponsor, vendor, funder, or participant owns ICSN merely by building with it, funding it, or operating related infrastructure.

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not access, custody, control, transact, or otherwise exercise authority over M5Member private data or member-controlled value.

## Human authority first

M5-compatible participation environments are designed so that people and authorized organizations remain the primary authority over their eligible identity, credentials, private data, wallet relationships, digital-asset relationships, connected devices, AI agents, and delegated actions.

Technology serves the human being. A platform, algorithm, AI agent, device, wallet, network, or automated process does not become the independent source of authority merely because it stores data, computes a result, operates an interface, or executes a technical action.

## Core commitments

- Human and legitimate organizational authority
- Privacy, confidentiality, and meaningful consent
- Member control of eligible private data and value relationships
- Portability, recovery, and practical exit
- Scoped, accountable, and revocable delegated authority
- Credentialed, attributable, and auditable participation
- Independent standards governance and sponsor independence
- Technology neutrality and cryptographic agility
- Independent implementation and interoperability
- Clear separation between public registry commitments and private member information

## Important limitations

Use of an ICSN-compatible identifier, schema, API, registry, protocol, credential, wallet relationship, or participation profile does not itself establish legal identity, legal title, legal ownership, custody, authority to transfer, regulatory compliance, payment settlement, asset value, or the factual accuracy of a record.

Legal effect depends on applicable law, governing agreements, competent authority, evidence, and the facts of the particular matter.

## Foundation commons and M5Stack boundary

Publication of these standards does not grant access to restricted M5Stack implementations, production systems, private member or partner services, credentials, or special commercial licenses. See the [M5Ecosystem Approval Boundary](../M5ECOSYSTEM-APPROVAL-BOUNDARY.md).
