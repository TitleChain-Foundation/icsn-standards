# M5-CV Fair Matching

> **Status:** Public review draft.

Matching uses holder-approved M5-CV evidence and transparent SOPHIA role requirements to suggest contribution, learning, demonstration, or service pathways. It does not create an invisible human-worth score or silently activate an operating role.

## Required safeguards

- Use only purpose-relevant, holder-approved fields.
- Keep private identity and sensitive evidence outside the matching projection.
- Distinguish self-declared, evidence-backed, credential-backed, and verified claims.
- Explain which requirements and evidence produced a suggestion.
- Allow correction and human review.
- Do not infer protected or sensitive attributes for ranking.
- Do not treat “no current match” as a permanent label.
- A match never creates employment, professional authority, a credential, or M5AGT delegation.

For consequential operating roles, current authority is evaluated separately through the M5Canon Six-Gate model.
