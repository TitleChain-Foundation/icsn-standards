# Planned DUNA Commons Governance

> **Status:** Future governance roadmap. No DUNA is represented as currently formed or activated by this document.

TitleChain Foundation's current legal and operating entities remain responsible for their existing obligations while the public standards and commons infrastructure are developed.

Upon:

- funding of the team needed to support the standards program;
- activation of the required member base;
- adoption of governing principles;
- establishment of administrator and stewardship controls; and
- completion of appropriate legal, tax, regulatory, and California operating review,

the Foundation intends to organize a **separate Wyoming Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (DUNA)** for decentralized stewardship and member governance of eligible TitleChain/ICSN public-commons activities.

## Wyoming legal framework

The Wyoming DUNA framework currently requires at least 100 members joined by mutual consent under an agreement for a common nonprofit purpose and an election to operate under the DUNA Act. Governing principles may include agreements, consensus mechanisms, smart contracts, and enacted governance proposals.

A DUNA is a distinct unincorporated nonprofit association. This roadmap does not state that the existing TitleChain corporation, Foundation, or M5Labs automatically converts into a DUNA.

Official source:
- Wyoming Statutes, Title 17, Chapter 32: https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title17.pdf
- 2026 Session Laws amendments: https://wyoleg.gov/2026/SessionLaws.pdf

## Human-rooted governance

The planned commons governance follows the same authority rule as the technical architecture:

```text
M5HUM / lawful member or administrator
        ↓
current governance role / authority
        ↓
governing principles
        ↓
M5Canon policy
        ↓
bounded M5AGT, if any
        ↓
approved commons action
        ↓
evidence / audit
```

**Membership alone does not confer operational authority.**

Administrator authority, regulated roles, credentials, delegated agent permissions, treasury permissions, standards stewardship, and executable actions remain separately defined, scoped, revocable, and auditable.

## Relationship to existing entities

- **TitleChain Foundation** — public standards / stewardship organization and current legal entity responsible for its existing obligations.
- **TitleChain Foundation Labs (M5Labs)** — Beverly Hills operating/research/engineering arm supporting implementation, research, standards work, and program operations.
- **Planned DUNA** — future member-governed commons vehicle for eligible decentralized public-commons activities after formation requirements and legal review are satisfied.

Any later restructuring, delegation, contracting, asset transfer, IP license, employment relationship, tax election, or regulated activity must be separately authorized and documented.

## Non-capture purpose

The DUNA roadmap is intended to strengthen the Foundation's long-term non-capture model by giving eligible commons activities a member-governed structure while preserving accountable human authority, public standards, independent implementation, and the legal responsibilities of regulated actors.
