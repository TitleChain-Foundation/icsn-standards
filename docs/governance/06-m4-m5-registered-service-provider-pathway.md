## 1. Nature of the pathway

The M4–M5 Registered Service Provider Pathway is an implementation-specific directory, credential, and interoperability framework for independent providers.

M4–M5 labels are internal participation classifications. They are not government licensing categories, regulatory designations, legal authorizations, endorsements, certifications, or guarantees.

## 2. Separation from Foundation and ICSN

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not provide M4–M5 services, do not custody member data or value, and do not act as an intermediary in transactions between members and providers.

Each provider operates independently and remains solely responsible for the services it offers.

## 3. Provider registration requirements

A provider seeking an M4–M5 listing or credential MUST provide, as applicable:

- Verified legal-entity identity
- Jurisdiction of formation and registration
- Authorized-representative credentials
- Applicable professional, financial, custody, payment, exchange, data, or operational licenses and registrations
- Insurance or bonding information where relevant
- Security and privacy documentation
- Customer terms and disclosures
- Complaint and escalation contacts
- Service categories and geographic limitations
- Credential status and revocation method
- Evidence supporting stated authorization or qualification

## 4. Scope of reliance

An M4–M5 provider credential confirms only the scope expressly stated in the credential and current directory or registry record.

Members, relying parties, and other providers MUST NOT infer authorization beyond verified category, jurisdiction, credential scope, or stated operating conditions.

## 5. Member choice and consent

An M5Member chooses whether to engage a provider.

The member MUST authorize disclosure of member-controlled information to a provider, except where another lawful basis applies.

The provider remains responsible for obtaining any consent, agreement, verification, authorization, and compliance evidence required for its own services.

## 6. No Foundation guarantee

An M4–M5 listing, credential, directory entry, interoperability profile, standards reference, or technical integration does not constitute a recommendation, endorsement, warranty, guarantee, insurance, supervision, assumption of liability, agency relationship, legal approval, or regulatory approval by TitleChain Foundation or ICSN.

## 7. Ongoing status

Providers SHOULD maintain current registration, credential, licensing, security, privacy, contact, complaint, and jurisdictional-scope information.

A provider may be suspended or removed from the directory under a documented process for expiration, loss of eligibility, false representation, material security concern, or other published grounds, subject to fair process and lawful restrictions.
