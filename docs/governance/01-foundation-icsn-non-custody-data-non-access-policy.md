# Foundation and ICSN Non-Custody and Data Non-Access Policy

> **Publication status:** Adopted Version 1.0  
> **Adopted by:** TitleChain Foundation authorized governance body  
> **Effective date:** 2026-08-28  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-28  
> **Next scheduled review:** 2027-08-28  
> **Legal review:** Approved for publication by authorized counsel on 2026-08-28.

## 1. Core commitment

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not own, custody, control, access, receive, hold, transmit, exchange, transact, pledge, encumber, transfer, settle, invest, administer, or otherwise exercise authority over M5Member private data, private records, private keys, recovery secrets, wallet contents, digital assets, fiat value, tokenized value, or other member-controlled value.

The Foundation and ICSN are standards, governance, interoperability, and public-specification functions. They are not member custodians, wallet operators, payment intermediaries, exchanges, brokers, dealers, banks, trust companies, investment advisers, money transmitters, asset managers, or financial institutions, unless a separately organized and duly authorized entity expressly undertakes a particular role under its own terms, licenses, registrations, and applicable law.

## 2. No access to member-private environments

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not have access to M5Member private data, M5POD contents, M5Vault contents, private credentials, private messages, wallet private keys, seed phrases, recovery secrets, signing secrets, authentication secrets, or decrypted private records.

The Foundation and ICSN MUST NOT request, collect, store, process, retain, transmit, sell, lease, share, disclose, analyze, train on, or otherwise use those materials.

The Foundation and ICSN may process only the limited public, administrative, governance, community, security, legal-contact, or technical information reasonably necessary to operate their own public standards functions, as disclosed in an applicable privacy notice and subject to applicable law.

## 3. No custody or control of value

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not custody, control, hold, transmit, exchange, settle, or take possession of member value.

Neither the Foundation nor ICSN has authority to initiate a transaction, move a member asset, sign with a member key, alter a member wallet, access a member vault, alter a member’s external-wallet association, or approve, reject, freeze, seize, or release a member’s value.

Any value-related activity occurs, if at all, exclusively between the M5Member and a separately registered M4–M5 provider acting in its own independent legal, technical, contractual, and regulatory capacity.

## 4. Member-controlled environment

An M5Member retains primary control over the member’s eligible M5POD, M5Vault, M5Wallet relationship, credentials, keys, recovery arrangements, data-sharing permissions, external-wallet associations, and M5 Agent delegations, subject to the member’s chosen configuration, applicable service terms, applicable law, and the technical limitations of any external wallet, custody provider, device, or network.

No reference to a member-controlled environment constitutes a guarantee of legal ownership, beneficial ownership, legal title, custody, authority to transfer, asset value, transaction finality, or regulatory status.

## 5. Registered M4–M5 pathways

M4–M5 service providers may offer specialized, regulated, high-assurance, or value-related services through independently registered pathways, subject to applicable eligibility, credential, licensing, contractual, security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

Each provider is independently responsible for its legal authority, licenses and registrations, service obligations, data practices, security, incident response, customer disclosures, complaint handling, and compliance with applicable law.

Registration in an M5 or ICSN-related directory, registry, profile, or credential system does not constitute endorsement, certification, licensing, supervision, insurance, guarantee, legal approval, regulatory approval, or agency relationship by TitleChain Foundation or ICSN.

## 6. Notice routing only

A TitleChain-compatible or M5-compatible notice mechanism may route an authenticated notice to an M5Member Notice Channel.

Routing, recording, timestamping, or delivering a notice does not:

- Grant the sender, the Foundation, ICSN, or any other party access to a member’s data, M5POD, M5Vault, M5Wallet, keys, credentials, agents, or value
- Establish legal service, legal authority, legal jurisdiction, legal ownership, legal title, custody, or entitlement
- Authorize a transaction, transfer, restriction, seizure, disclosure, or other action
- Determine whether a notice is legally valid, enforceable, or sufficient

## 7. Technical separation requirements

The Foundation and ICSN SHOULD maintain technical, administrative, contractual, and organizational separation from member-controlled data and value environments.

At minimum, Foundation and ICSN systems MUST NOT possess:

- Member private keys
- Seed phrases or equivalent recovery secrets
- Member signing authority
- Member wallet transaction authority
- Decryption keys for member private records
- Administrative backdoor access to private M5POD or M5Vault content
- Omnibus custody accounts holding member value
- Authority to alter, suspend, reassign, or transfer member-controlled assets

## 8. Public-record limitation

A public standards or registry function may store only the minimum non-sensitive information necessary to support a published standard, such as a public identifier, cryptographic commitment, public-key reference, schema reference, status reference, timestamp, non-sensitive event type, or public conformance record.

A public record MUST NOT be represented as a copy of, substitute for, or access path to private member data or member-controlled value.

## 9. No agency relationship

Nothing in ICSN, TitleChain Foundation governance, an M5 profile, registry record, credential, directory listing, standards conformance claim, or notice-routing function creates a partnership, joint venture, fiduciary relationship, custodial relationship, agency relationship, employment relationship, broker relationship, banking relationship, trust relationship, or other legal relationship between TitleChain Foundation or ICSN and an M5Member or M4–M5 provider, except as expressly stated in a separate written agreement signed by authorized parties.

## 10. Interpretation

This policy states a standards and operational-separation principle. It does not waive, limit, or alter obligations that may arise under applicable law, a valid legal order, an independently executed agreement, or a separately operated and legally responsible service-provider relationship.
