### Key policy determinations

- TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not access, custody, control, transact, or otherwise exercise authority over M5Member private data or member-controlled value.
- M5 participation requires attributable, active, credentialed authority through an M5Member or registered M5 Agency or Organization relationship and an active M5Wallet relationship.
- AI agents, devices, wallets, and automated systems are technical tools and delegates; they are not independent sources of authority over a person or organization.
- M5 notice routing is an authenticated, auditable “You Have Been Served” process and does not itself establish legal service, legal authority, access to member data, or access to member-controlled value.
- M4–M5 providers are independently operated and separately accountable for their regulated, financial, custody, payment, exchange, or other specialized services.
- M1–M5 is published as a proposed classification and interoperability taxonomy, not as an official currency code, government code, legal tender, or regulatory designation.
