# Instructor and Curriculum Activation

> **Status:** Public Review Draft.

The Foundation organizes people who can teach, mentor, review, and build SOPHIA learning pathways. The process starts with an accountable person, holder-controlled evidence, and transparent role requirements rather than an automated résumé score.

```text
M5IAM → TCID/M5HUM → M5POD → M5-CV → SOPHIA → review / credential → learning or bounded role
```

## Pathway

1. Review the public work.
2. Use M5IAM if choosing to activate the M5 reference environment.
3. Keep private identity/evidence in the M5POD or another conforming private store.
4. Maintain holder-selected M5-CV capability/contribution evidence.
5. Select or propose SOPHIA roles.
6. Propose or attend curriculum with clear outcomes, prerequisites, accessibility, and assessment.
7. Receive human/domain review and any required credential from the accountable issuer.
8. Activate an operating role only after the applicable M5Canon authority gates pass.

A self-declared capability is not an issued credential. Teaching interest does not create appointment, employment, payment, membership, professional status, or agent authority.
