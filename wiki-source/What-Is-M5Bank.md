# What Is M5Bank?

> **Publication status:** Adopted Version 1.0  
> **Adopted by:** TitleChain Foundation authorized governance body  
> **Effective date:** 2026-08-28  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-28  
> **Next scheduled review:** 2027-08-28  
> **Legal review:** Approved for publication by authorized counsel on 2026-08-28.

M5Bank is a member-controlled financial and digital-agency environment for people and registered or regulated operating entities.

It is designed to help members participate in the world, market, and digital economy with their own credentials, private data, wallet relationships, digital-asset relationships, AI agents, connected devices, notices, records, and accountable authority.

## Member-controlled financial agency

M5Bank is built around **Member-Controlled Financial Agency**: a design principle in which an M5Member or registered organization remains the primary decision-maker over eligible identity, credentials, private data, wallet relationships, digital-asset relationships, AI agents, devices, permissions, and interactions with independently operated financial or regulated service providers.

Member control does not mean exemption from applicable law, contractual obligations, fraud controls, sanctions requirements, court orders, network rules, or the independent terms and eligibility requirements of a provider.

## What members control

An M5Member remains the primary authority over the member’s eligible:

- Identity and credential relationships
- Private data and disclosure permissions
- M5POD and M5Vault relationships
- M5Wallet and supported external-wallet relationships
- Digital-asset relationship records
- AI-agent permissions and revocations
- Connected-device associations
- Notice preferences and authenticated communications
- Delegated authority to registered organizations and service providers

## Financial and regulated services

TitleChain Foundation and ICSN do not access, custody, control, or transact member data or value.

Where banking, payment, custody, exchange, lending, investment, insurance, legal, identity, or other regulated services are available, those services are provided only by separately registered, credentialed, and independently accountable M4–M5 service providers acting within their own legal, contractual, technical, and regulatory authority.

A member chooses whether to engage a provider and authorizes the data sharing and permissions required for that specific service.

## Human-first AI and technology

AI agents, connected devices, applications, wallets, sensors, and other technologies are tools of the member or authorized organization.

They may act only within the scope the member or organization has granted, and they must be attributable, auditable, reviewable, and revocable.

No platform, algorithm, agent, or device becomes sovereign authority over a person merely because it stores data, calculates a score, manages an interface, or performs an automated action.

## Due notice and communications

M5Bank supports authenticated and attributable notice routing through a member-controlled Notice Channel.

A routed notice does not grant the sender, TitleChain Foundation, ICSN, or any other party access to a member’s private data, M5POD, M5Vault, wallet, credentials, devices, agents, or value.

M5Bank does not determine legal rights, legal jurisdiction, ownership, title, custody, or the legal effect of a notice. Those matters remain subject to applicable law, governing agreements, competent authority, and the responsibility of independently operated service providers.

## Short statement

**M5Bank is self-sovereign banking in practice: a member-controlled financial and digital-agency environment where people and authorized organizations govern their identity, credentials, private data, wallet relationships, AI agents, devices, and delegated authority—while independently operated providers deliver services through their own accountable pathways.**
