# M5 Due Notice and Credentialed Authority Policy

> **Publication status:** Adopted Version 1.0  
> **Adopted by:** TitleChain Foundation authorized governance body  
> **Effective date:** 2026-08-28  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-28  
> **Next scheduled review:** 2027-08-28  
> **Legal review:** Approved for publication by authorized counsel on 2026-08-28.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes an attributable, authenticated, auditable, and notice-first process for requests that may concern an M5Member account, M5Wallet relationship, M5POD, M5Vault, M5 Agent, credential, digital-asset relationship, or associated authority record.

The policy is designed to ensure that material requests are not routed anonymously, informally, or without a documented basis.

## 2. Due Notice principle

Except where immediate action is required by applicable law, a valid emergency policy, a binding legal order, a credible security incident, a fraud-prevention requirement, a sanctions requirement, or another documented mandatory obligation, the designated notice-routing operator SHOULD provide the affected M5Member or registered M5 Agency or Organization with a Due Notice before routing or recording a material request affecting the participant’s recorded relationships.

This workflow is called **You Have Been Served**.

A Due Notice is a platform notice and evidence record. It is not, by itself:

- Legal service of process
- A court order
- A seizure, lien, judgment, or administrative order
- Proof of ownership or authority
- A determination of legal rights

## 3. Credentialed requester requirement

A requesting party seeking to use a designated notice-routing interface MUST be identifiable, attributable, and authenticated through an approved credentialed identity and authority process.

A request MUST identify:

- The requester
- Credential issuer and status
- Legal, contractual, administrative, security, or policy basis
- Affected relationship
- Requested action or response
- Asserted jurisdiction or governing-law basis
- Effective date and urgency basis
- Supporting materials
- Secure response channel

Authentication of a requester does not establish that its request is valid, sufficient, enforceable, applicable, or entitled to a response.

## 4. Eligible issuer categories

Subject to verification, a notice-routing system may recognize credentials issued by:

- An authorized business-registration authority
- A public agency
- A Department of Human Services or equivalent body
- A DMV or equivalent credentialing authority
- A court or tribunal
- An authorized judicial officer
- A tribal government or tribal court
- A national, state, provincial, local, municipal, or territorial authority
- A licensed professional or service provider
- A registered M5 Agency or Organization
- Another issuer approved under a published trust policy

Recognition of an issuer does not establish that every credential it issues is current, applicable, sufficient, legally effective, or authorized for the request at issue.

## 5. Notice-routing workflow

A notice-routing operator may:

- Receive and timestamp a request
- Authenticate a requesting party
- Verify technical credential status
- Identify the intended member or organization
- Route the notice to the designated Notice Channel
- Provide a delivery or access receipt where configured and lawful
- Preserve an integrity record of the notice event

A notice-routing operator MUST NOT:

- Provide a requester access to member private data
- Determine the legal merits of a notice
- Determine that legal service occurred
- Alter member authority, wallet, agent, credential, data, or value
- Direct an independent provider to act
- Create a legal duty beyond applicable law and separately agreed terms

## 6. No self-executing asset access

A request, notice, credential, claim, invoice, complaint, allegation, lien assertion, demand letter, registry entry, or platform message MUST NOT by itself authorize the Foundation, ICSN, or a notice-routing function to:

- Transfer a digital asset
- Transfer control of a controllable electronic record
- Disclose secrets or vault contents
- Change an M5Wallet controller
- Grant access to an M5POD or M5Vault
- Override security controls
- Reassign a credential or authority record
- Determine ownership, title, custody, beneficial ownership, or legal authority

A party seeking an operational response from a regulated, custodial, payment, exchange, or other value-service provider must submit its request directly to that independently operated provider through the provider’s own authorized legal, contractual, and compliance process.

## 7. Emergency and mandatory exceptions

A notice-routing operator may apply limited technical measures, including delayed routing or preservation of an integrity record, only where necessary to meet a documented mandatory obligation, binding legal process applicable to the operator, or credible security threat.

The operator SHOULD provide notice as soon as legally and operationally permitted and MUST document the basis, scope, time, decision authority, and review status of the exception.

## 8. Review and correction

Where permitted by law and policy, an affected participant SHOULD have access to a process for correction, response, dispute, or escalation concerning inaccurate routing metadata or platform-status information.

## 9. Recordkeeping

The operator SHOULD maintain an auditable record of:

- Request identifier
- Requester identity and technical credential result
- Claimed basis
- Intended recipient
- Notice and delivery status
- Response metadata, if any
- Relevant policy version
- Retention status

Recordkeeping remains subject to privacy, security, and lawful-retention limits.

## 10. No legal-effect representation

This policy does not determine the legal validity, legal effect, jurisdiction, ownership, custody, title, priority, enforceability, or sufficiency of a request, credential, notice, order, or asset relationship.
