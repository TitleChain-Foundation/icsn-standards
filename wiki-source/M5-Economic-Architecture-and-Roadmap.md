# M5 Economic Architecture — Human Authority, Real-World Assets, and Public Economic Intelligence

> **Status:** M5 implementation and roadmap reference. The public architecture does not grant governmental, banking, securities, or monetary authority and does not require use of M5 to participate in ICSN standards.

## One architecture, separate dimensions

M5 keeps several dimensions separate so one technical label cannot silently overwrite another:

```text
WHO IS ACTING
M5IAM / M5HUM / M5Bank account context

WHAT IS THE UNDERLYING RIGHT OR ASSET
TitleChain Title Container + M1/M2/M3 underlying class

WHAT FINANCIAL / REGULATORY STATE APPLIES
M4 financial wrapper + M5 jurisdictional security-state profile
+ external authoritative legal/regulatory state

WHAT MAY HAPPEN NOW
credentials + delegation + restrictions + jurisdiction + approvals
→ M5Canon Six-Gate evaluation

HOW AN AUTHORIZED INSTRUCTION EXECUTES
M5-OMI + replaceable banking/payment/settlement/chain rails

WHAT EVIDENCE AND ECONOMIC SIGNAL REMAINS
TitleChain provenance + M5 Ledger + M5 Global Index and Exchange
```

## M1–M5 asset/economic state model

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **M1** | Money + Utility |
| **M2** | Commodities |
| **M3** | Titled / Unique / Collectible RWA |
| **M4** | Financial Wrapper |
| **M5** | Jurisdictional Security-State Profile |

M4/M5 are additive states/profiles. They do not erase the underlying M1/M2/M3 Title Container/class.

External SEC/CFTC, banking, tax, property, or other legal classifications remain separate authoritative projections. M5Canon may enforce a supplied/established legal state; it does not create a legal conclusion from an internal label.

## M5HUM → M5AGT → M5AST → M5MST

- **M5HUM — Human Principal.** Credentialed human sovereign. Every M5AGT is accountable to a named M5HUM.
- **M5AGT — Autonomous Agent.** AI agent acting on behalf of a human. It cannot exceed the M5HUM's permissions.
- **M5AST — Asset Token.** Real-world asset-backed token with one of five M1–M5 tiers on WyomingChain.eth.
- **M5MST — Asset Minting.** Asset-minting event or TitleChain registration on WyomingChain.eth.

An M5MST event records minting or registration; it does not itself grant transfer authority. Mint authority and transfer authority are distinct.

## TitleChain Title Container

The Title Container preserves the underlying right/asset, origin, provenance, ownership/control evidence, rights/obligations, jurisdiction, liens/notices/encumbrances, authorized digital representations, restrictions, corrections, and lifecycle events.

A token is not presumed to be the underlying asset or the legally authoritative ownership record.

## M5Canon Six-Gate authority

Consequential state changes resolve through six control requirements:

1. accountable principal identity;
2. current role/credential;
3. jurisdiction;
4. deterministic policy/restrictions;
5. required accountable approval; and
6. evidence/audit anchoring.

An M5AGT cannot activate beyond the current authority of its M5HUM/legal principal.

## Jurisdiction / JNR

M5Global/JNR resolves technical jurisdiction namespaces and identifiers to authoritative external sources. The M5 reference implementation maintains 170+ jurisdiction-chain ENS namespaces as technical resolution references. Those names do not create governmental authority or control official DNS/.gov resources.

## SwiftBRIDGE

SwiftBRIDGE maps legacy financial-institution identifiers/account-routing relationships to M5 entity, jurisdiction, and account references while preserving the original authoritative identifier source. It is not the SWIFT network and does not imply SWIFT/BIS endorsement.

## M5-OMI

M5-OMI is the implementation-neutral boundary that moves an **authorized** economic instruction into replaceable banking, payment, token, stablecoin, tokenized-deposit, custody, messaging, and settlement rails. The rail is not authoritative over the accountable principal, Title Container, jurisdictional meaning, or legally controlling ownership record.

## M5 Global Index and Exchange

The M5 Global Index and Exchange is the public economic-intelligence/index-data layer of M5Global. It is designed to make privacy-preserving, provenance-aware indicators and methodologies reviewable by agencies, economists, statisticians, researchers, standards communities, and asset authorities.

M5GPI and GGP are indicator/methodology families within this layer. “Exchange” means data/methodology/signal exchange, not a regulated trading venue.

## Public commons and implementation boundary

TitleChain Foundation / ICSN publish public standards, schemas, threat models, conformance requirements, and reference architecture. Restricted production code, private member data, keys, confidential integrations, and private registry records remain outside the public commons.

Open standards do not eliminate regulated roles. They make authority, provenance, restrictions, and evidence more explicit and portable.
