# Consent v0.4 Conformance Plan

Required tests:
- no disclosure without consent
- withdrawal stops future use
- revocation propagates
- correction produces updated state
- expired grant is denied
- non-biometric route remains usable where required
- private evidence never becomes public registry payload
- agent cannot extend its own scope
- consent for one purpose does not authorize another
- organizational authorization does not grant private M1/M5POD access
- deletion prevents future ordinary retrieval
- recovery does not restore revoked rights automatically

Execution evidence is required before claiming conformance.
