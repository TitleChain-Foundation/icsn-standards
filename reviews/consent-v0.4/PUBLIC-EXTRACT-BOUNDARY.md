# Public Extract Boundary — Consent v0.4

The controlled consent standard remains private until separately approved for disclosure.

A future public vendor-independent extract may include only approved terminology, rights-holder principles, state semantics, consent lifecycle rules, machine-readable field definitions, conformance expectations and implementation-neutral examples.

Do not publish biometric records, identity documents, protected-person data, private account identifiers, precise member location, private evidence, recovery secrets, production credentials or controlled legal analysis.
