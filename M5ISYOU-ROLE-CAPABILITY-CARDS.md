# M5isYOU Initial Role-Capability Cards

> **Status: Public Review Draft for the people's review.** These cards provide an initial transparent map from capability evidence to Foundation role families. They are not appointments, job descriptions or automated selection rules.

## How to read a card

- **Outcomes** describe the work to be accomplished.
- **Core capabilities** normally require evidence or an accessible demonstration.
- **Developable capabilities** may be learned through a cohort, mentor or bounded assignment.
- **Possible evidence** is deliberately broader than paid employment or formal education.

No card may use the prohibited inputs in [M5isYOU Fair Matching](M5ISYOU-FAIR-MATCHING.md).

## 1. Canon and sovereign systems

- **Outcomes:** coherent M5Canon decisions, modular architecture and controlled change.
- **Core capabilities:** I can strategize; I can design; I can govern; I can analyze; I can steward.
- **Developable:** M5 architecture vocabulary, decision records and conformance process.
- **Possible evidence:** system architecture, governance design, complex-program stewardship, standards work or documented resolution of architectural conflict.

## 2. Activation, operations and integration

- **Outcomes:** dependencies are visible, services have owners and launches meet readiness gates.
- **Core capabilities:** I can organize; I can operate; I can lead; I can strategize; I can connect.
- **Developable:** M5 account, registry and cohort workflows.
- **Possible evidence:** program operations, launch management, incident coordination, logistics, community operations or multi-team delivery.

## 3. Legal, jurisdiction and arbitration

- **Outcomes:** legally legible structures, qualified review and court-ready processes.
- **Core capabilities:** I can work with law; I can research; I can analyze; I can govern; I can ledger.
- **Developable:** M5 jurisdiction-chain and `.pol` workflow.
- **Possible evidence:** legal research, registry operations, contracts, policy, tribunal administration, comparative law or current professional qualification.

## 4. Security, privacy, risk and audit

- **Core capabilities:** I can secure; I can test and challenge; I can manage risk; I can analyze; I can steward.

- **Possible evidence:** security engineering, cryptography, privacy review, audit, safety, incident response, insurance or control testing.

## 5. Cohorts, learning and member success

## 6. Registry, identity and swiftBRIDGE

## Evolving the cards

A card change should explain:

- which work outcome changed;
- evidence supporting the change;
- communities likely to be affected;
- whether the change creates a new barrier;
- accessibility and bias considerations; and
- its effective date and version.

People may propose capabilities or evidence types missing from a card. The vocabulary must remain open to lived and emerging forms of expertise.
