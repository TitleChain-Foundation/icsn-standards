# Initial Reference Sources

**Prepared:** 2026-09-09

These sources motivate the first public reference case. They do not define the project methodology and should not be treated as proof of behavior across all models or vendors.

## 1. Gamers Nexus — Data Dragnet

Project/funding page:

https://store.gamersnexus.net/data-dragnet

The page describes a broader consumer-device privacy investigation, including LG smart TVs, local-network device identification, ACR, microphone/privacy questions, and plans to investigate additional smart-TV manufacturers.

**Evidence status:** researcher/publisher stated. Individual technical claims require the underlying investigation evidence and independent reproduction before being promoted to Commons findings.

## 2. Ars Technica — LG TV shown scanning LAN for third-party phones and other devices

Published September 8, 2026:

https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/lg-tv-shown-capable-of-tracking-user-activity-even-when-offline/

Ars reports on the Gamers Nexus / Level1Techs work and includes LG's response. The article describes packet-capture evidence of LAN discovery and reports the researchers' audio/local-storage demonstration. It also quotes LG explaining that local-network scanning supports smart-TV functionality and that wake-word audio processing is local under enabled Far-Field functionality, with LG disputing the characterization that TVs collect/record/transmit ambient conversations outside intentionally activated voice functionality.

**Evidence status:** secondary reporting + vendor stated. Use the original technical evidence for technical findings where available.

## 3. Texas Attorney General — LG ACR agreement

Published May 11, 2026:

https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-ken-paxton-secures-major-agreement-lg-protect-texans-privacy-and-stop-data-being

The Texas Attorney General states that an agreement with LG requires informed consent before ACR viewing-data collection, clearer disclosure, and a simple opt-out mechanism.

**Evidence status:** regulator stated.

## Source handling rule

For every public Commons record:

- link the source;
- date it;
- identify source type;
- distinguish observation from interpretation;
- include vendor response where material;
- include regulator statement separately;
- never convert a viral headline into a measured finding without evidence.
