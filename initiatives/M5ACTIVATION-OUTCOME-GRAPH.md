# M5 Activation Outcome Graph

> **Program measurement framework.** Example numbers below illustrate the reporting structure; they are not claimed program results.

## Why measure activation, not attendance

The objective is not to report how many people watched a class.

The objective is to understand whether sponsored members moved through a real activation pathway while preserving member ownership and privacy.

A sponsor should be able to understand outcomes such as:

> **You funded 100 member activations, and the program produced a measurable number of activated Freedom Offices, credentialed capabilities, economic outcomes, trainers, businesses, and completed workflows.**

The individual evidence belongs to the member.

TitleChain Foundation / the applicable program operator may report **aggregate program outcomes** without turning private member data into the product.

## Example outcome graph

```text

    ↓

    ↓
81 completed the defined activation
    ↓
63 earned approved credentials / capability evidence
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

```

These figures are an **illustrative reporting pattern only**.

Each published impact report should show the actual period, cohort, definitions, denominator, evidence standard, and privacy threshold used.

## Core outcome states

### Sponsored
A funded seat or activation allocation was made available under the applicable program criteria.

### Activated
The member completed the defined minimum technical/member activation for the reporting period.

The definition should distinguish **free IAM activation at m5bank.app** from **M5Member program enrollment at m5member.netlify.app**. For a program cohort, an activated state may include completed enrollment, M5BankofMe activation, Passport, and the applicable M5 Freedom Office baseline, according to the published metric definition.

### Completed
The member completed the defined cohort or activation requirements.

### Credentialed
The member received approved evidence or credentials under the applicable published criteria.

Course attendance alone should not automatically equal credentialing.

### Earning
The member reached a verified economic outcome selected for the program measure, such as a paid role, contract, service, venture revenue, or other approved earning event.

Aggregate reporting should not expose private income or transaction detail.

### Trainer / Activator
The member satisfied the applicable trainer or activator requirements and is authorized under current program rules to support others.

### Business activated
A member-established or member-operated business reached the defined activation state.

### Workflow completed
A scoped workflow was completed with the required human approval, agent activity, and evidence.

## Member evidence vs. sponsor reporting

The model separates **individual proof** from **aggregate impact**.

```text
Member
  owns / controls
  ↓
Passport + M5-CV + credentials + evidence + work history
                          │
                          │ privacy-preserving aggregation
                          ↓
Program impact layer
  ↓
activation counts + completion + credentials + earning outcomes
+ trainer outcomes + businesses + workflows
                          ↓
Sponsor report
```

Sponsors should not receive:

- private M5Brain contents;
- individual agent conversations;
- private Passport data;
- private M5-CV evidence;
- wallet or transaction histories;
- private business files;
- unnecessary demographic profiles; or
- unrestricted access to members.

## Sponsor impact statement

A sponsor report can say:

> **This sponsorship funded 100 activation opportunities. During the reporting period, X members activated the defined environment, Y completed the program, Z earned approved capability evidence, A reached verified economic outcomes, B qualified as trainers/activators, C businesses were activated, and D scoped workflows were completed. Individual evidence remains controlled by the member.**

This is materially more useful than reporting only:

> "We donated to a training program."

## The progression graph

The member's own progression is:

```text
I AM
  ↓
I KNOW
  ↓
I CAN
  ↓
I DID
  ↓
I CAN TEACH
  ↓
I CAN EARN
```

The program should measure movement through those states without reducing the human to a score.

## The compounding activation flywheel

```text
1 member activates
    ↓
she learns to operate her native M5Agents
    ↓
her Passport / M5-CV records approved capability evidence
    ↓
she enters a role, venture, or specialist track
    ↓
she proves competency through real work
    ↓
she earns
    ↓
if qualified, she becomes an activator / trainer
    ↓
she helps activate the next members
    ↓
some become trainers
    ↓
the network compounds
```

The objective is a distributed network of:

**operators + professionals + trainers + developers + entrepreneurs**

not a permanently centralized online school.

## Privacy floor

Program measurement should follow these principles:

- collect only what is necessary to prove the defined outcome;
- separate sponsor records from member data;
- report aggregates rather than private member evidence;
- use minimum cohort/privacy thresholds where appropriate;
- avoid behavioral advertising and cross-context profiling;
- let the member control the underlying Passport, M5-CV, credentials, and evidence;
- document metric definitions before claiming results; and
- never sell program participation data as a sponsor benefit.

## Sponsor principle

> **Fund the activation. Measure the outcome. Never own the member.**
