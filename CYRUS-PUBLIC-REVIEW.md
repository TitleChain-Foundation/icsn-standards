# Cyrus Commons License 1.0 — Public Review Draft

The Cyrus Purpose-Bound Constitutional Commons License, Version 1.0, is
published for review by people, communities, developers, implementers,
researchers, institutions, public-interest organizations, and other affected
participants.

Public review is intended to test whether the license and Purpose Covenant are
legible, implementable, resistant to capture, respectful of human dignity,
privacy and consent, and suitable for durable public-interest infrastructure.

Reviewers are invited to examine:

- whether permissions and restrictions are understandable;
- whether independent and commercial implementation remains practical;
- whether privacy, consent, fair process, accountability, and non-capture
  protections are specific enough to evaluate;
- whether contribution, provenance, designation, suspension, cure, and
  termination processes are workable;
- whether affected people and communities have meaningful review and
  escalation paths; and
- whether any term creates unintended exclusion, ambiguity, dependency, or
  concentrated authority.

Publication as a Public Review Draft does not make the text an approved ICSN
standard, a certification, an endorsement of any implementation, or authority
to access private systems, data, credentials, registries, financial services,
trademarks, or production infrastructure.

Feedback belongs in public Discussions unless it contains confidential,
personal, security-sensitive, or restricted information. Sensitive security
findings must use private vulnerability reporting.
